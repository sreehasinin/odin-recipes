Pizza Recipe

Description

The pizza is a classic dish loved by many. This pizza is a cheesy, juicy and super delicious dish that you would not want to miss! Whether its the stretchy mozzerella cheese, or the lovely aroma of oregano, there is something for everyone to love in this pizza. Be sure to follow along to make a really, really delicious pizza!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup warm water
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp dry active yeast
  • a pinch of salt
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp pizza sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzerella cheese
  • choice of toppings
  • 1/2 tsp oregano

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, combine the warm water, sugar and dry yeast. Rest for 10 minutes. Then add the flour, salt and olive oil. Knead until there are no clumps. Cover the bowl with a damp cloth and let it rise for atleast 1-2 hours.
  2. Place the dough on a lightly dusted surface, and stretch or roll it into the size of your choice (we recommend 12 inches). transer the dough into a baking tray (lined) and poke holes in the dough for air bubbles to escape. Create a thick border for more crust.
  3. Spread the pizza sauce evenly and gently on the dough. Sprinkle the mozzerella cheese onto the dough, and top it off with your favourite toppings. Finish with oregano.
  4. Preheat oven to maximum temperature. Bake for 12- 15 minutes or until the crust turns golden brown.
  5. And done!
