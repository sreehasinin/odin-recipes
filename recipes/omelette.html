Omelette Recipe

Description

A classic breakfast dish! Who doesnt know the omelette? a healthy, protein rich meal is a great way to start your day. Follow the recipe down below to take your omelette making game to the next level!

Ingredients

  • 3 large eggs
  • 1/2 tbsp butter
  • salt and pepper
  • fillings (optional)

Steps

  1. Crack the eggs into a large bowl. Add the salt and pepper (to your liking) and whisk the mixture until frothy.
  2. Place a non-stick pan on medium heat and melt the butter.
  3. Pour the whisked eggs into the pan and leave undisturbed for 10-15 seconds.
  4. Use a silione spatula to flip the edges of the omelette towards the center. Tilt the pan to spread the liquid mixture to the edges. Keep repeating until the omelette looks set.
  5. Add your desired toppings onto one half of the omelette.
  6. Gently fold the omelette in half and serve.
  7. Done!
