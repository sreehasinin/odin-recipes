Pancake Recipe

Description

A sweet way to start your day! There's nothing like a sweet pancake on a bad morning! With its spongy goodness and sticky syrup, it's on top of the breakfast list! Tune in to make the best pancakes you will ever eat!

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp white sugar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, salt and sugar until well combined.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the milk, melted butter, egg and vanilla extract.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Stir gently until just combined. Let the batter rest for 5-10 minutes.
  4. Scoop 1/4 cup of batter for each pancake. Cook until you see bubbles then flip. Cook for another 1-2 minutes.
  5. Serve with toppings of your choice.
  6. Enjoy!
